A little about us

Squeezed from real lemons.

We’re a family-owned Chicago spot with one simple idea: great lemon juice. We squeeze every glass to order, using ripe lemons and limes, fresh mint and just enough sweetness.

Stop by for something cool and citrusy, and stay as long as you like.
